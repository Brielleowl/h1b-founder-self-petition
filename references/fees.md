# Fees (verify at https://www.uscis.gov/g-1055 before quoting)

Amounts below are the April 2024 fee rule as in force in 2026, with the premium-processing fee
after its 1 March 2026 adjustment. Always re-check G-1055 and the premium processing page; the
online form computes the total itself — compare, don't assume.

## Small petitioner (25 or fewer full-time-equivalent employees, non-profit rules not applicable)

| Fee | Amount | Note |
|---|---|---|
| I-129 base filing fee (H-1B) | $460 | small-employer rate ($780 for 26+ FTE) |
| Asylum Program Fee | $300 | $600 for 26+ FTE; $0 for nonprofits |
| ACWIA fee (training) | $750 | $1,500 for 26+ FTE; waived only for certain nonprofits/universities and for a second extension by the same employer |
| Fraud Prevention and Detection fee | $500 | required on change of employer and first petition by the employer; not on an extension by the same employer |
| **I-129 total** | **$2,010** | |
| I-907 premium processing | $2,965 | optional; 15 business days |
| **Total with premium** | **$4,975** | |

## Fees that do not apply to a founder change-of-employer petition

- **Public Law 114-113 $4,000 fee** — only for employers with 50+ U.S. employees and >50% H-1B/L-1.
- **$100,000 H-1B proclamation fee** (Presidential Proclamation of Sept 19 2025) — applies to new
  petitions for beneficiaries outside the U.S. seeking entry; USCIS guidance exempts change of status,
  extension of stay and change of employer for beneficiaries already in the U.S. in valid status.
  Re-read the current USCIS guidance when the facts differ.
- **Fee waiver / reduction (Form I-912)** — not available for I-129.

## Who pays

The petitioner. The ACWIA fee and the fraud fee may not be paid by, deducted from, or reimbursed by
the beneficiary (20 CFR 655.731(c)(9), (c)(10)(ii)). Pay from the company account. A founder who
pays from a personal card is both a wage-deduction problem and a bookkeeping mess; the board
consent template resolves that fees are company expenses.

## FTE count

Count all employees (U.S. and abroad) of the petitioner and any affiliate; a brand-new company with
only the founder counts 0 or 1. Answer the online form's FTE question with the number you put in
`petitioner.employees_total`.

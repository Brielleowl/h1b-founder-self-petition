# Decisions from a real founder filing (filed and approved under premium processing, 2026)

A solo founder, 100% owner and sole director of a weeks-old Delaware C-corp, filed a change-of-
employer + extension petition for herself as a part-time AI security engineer and was approved in
11 business days with no RFE. These are the calls that were debated and how they came out. They are
precedent for defaults, not rules.

## Structure
- **Sole director + 100% owner + President/CEO is fine.** The 2025 rule removed the separate
  employer-employee control test; the petitioner needs legal presence, a bona fide offer and an LCA.
  No outside director, no "board controls the founder" fiction.
- **18 months, not 3 years.** Request start + 18 months − 1 day. The LCA was certified for 3 years;
  the mismatch is expected.
- **Part-time 25 h/wk at Level I** kept the wage obligation fundable from the founder's capital and
  matched a genuinely entry-level JD. Level I was not challenged.
- **80/20 split** (20 h specialty, 5 h owner duties), all hours paid. The regulation requires a
  majority on specialty duties; 80/20 leaves room without pretending the founder never fundraises.
- **Start date later than the LCA start** and about two weeks after the planned filing date, so
  payroll would begin cleanly after receipt. Offer letter: "start, or the date USCIS receives the
  petition, if later."

## Documents
- Offer letter only; the employment agreement was dropped as redundant. Every mention of an
  "employment agreement" then had to be removed from the support letter.
- JD: 0 years' experience (a first draft said 2 years and contradicted Level I).
- Degree requirement listed four related fields; the same sentence was pasted verbatim into JD,
  offer letter, support letter and the I-129.
- Support letter did not mention the former employer's separation date or the time left on the
  current I-94 — neither helps the petition. It did cite the prior approval notices and pay stubs.
- A reference letter from a former manager and an expert opinion letter were prepared and then
  **not** submitted; exhibits were re-lettered so nothing cited was missing.
- Funding shown as: founder's $50k contribution (already in the company account, balance page
  attached) + planned pilot revenue + planned pre-seed; business plan carried a "pre-seed delayed"
  contingency. No founder commitment letter, no personal bank statements, no third-party contract
  values.
- Business plan: 24 pages, future tense for unshipped product, TOC regenerated from source (the
  Word export had a stale TOC); pitch deck and website screenshots went in as "other evidence".
- Lease clause allowing home-based business without client visits was quoted in the BP and the
  lease uploaded.

## Form
- Rate of pay per year = hourly × 25 × 52, explained in Additional Information.
- DOL attestation exemption answered "Yes" (master's) — literal answer, no effect for a
  non-dependent employer.
- Status expiry = I-94 date on the I-797A (10 days after the I-797 end), I-94 number from the same notice.
- Draft-review gaps found and fixed before submission: current H period "To" not Present, A-Number
  blank, JD not uploaded, present-occupation text lost its first clause, Additional Information empty,
  field-of-study list shortened, former-employer city without state.
- I-907 filed through "Finish and continue to I-907"; one payment of $4,975.

## Payment
- First payment failed: company credit card had a spend limit below $4,975 → USCIS page HTTP 500,
  bank shows *Failed*, case stayed in Drafts. Raised the limit, paid again, submitted within minutes.

## After
- Receipt same day; approval email on business day 11 (before the online account updated); the
  "Consulate notified" line in the email was the consulate typed on the form, not a change in
  processing; I-797A with I-94 to the petition end date followed.

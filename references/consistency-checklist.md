# Cross-document consistency checklist

Run this after drafting and again right before the browser phase. Report as a table:
`item · documents checked · values found · fix`. Everything below has broken in a real filing at
least once.

## Dates and period
- [ ] Start date identical in offer letter, support letter, job description, board consent, business
      plan ("period of intended employment") and I-129 requested period.
- [ ] Start ≥ LCA start; start ≥ filing date. Offer letter says "<start>, or the date USCIS receives
      the petition, if later" so a late filing does not create a gap.
- [ ] End ≤ LCA end; end − start ≤ 18 months (first beneficiary-owner petition).
- [ ] Support letter does **not** state the beneficiary's last day at the former employer or how much
      time remains on the current I-94 unless the user wants it.
- [ ] Business plan's 18-month funding table covers exactly start → end.

## Position terms (must equal the LCA)
- [ ] Job title, SOC code and title, wage level.
- [ ] Wage rate per hour; annualised figure = rate × hours × 52 everywhere it appears.
- [ ] Hours per week; part-time flag; hours split (specialty vs owner) identical in JD, offer letter,
      support letter, board consent, I-129 duties text. Specialty share > 50%.
- [ ] Worksite address, including unit number, identical to the LCA (letters, I-129, lease).
- [ ] Degree-requirement sentence identical in JD, offer letter, support letter, I-129.
- [ ] Experience requirement: Level I → "no prior work experience is required" everywhere; no
      "2+ years" anywhere.
- [ ] Supervisory duties: none; license: none — consistent with Level I.

## People and roles
- [ ] Beneficiary's legal name spelled as on passport in every document; preferred name only in
      "other names".
- [ ] Officer titles identical in support letter signature block, offer letter signature block,
      board consent, I-129 signer, I-907 contact ("President and Chief Executive Officer", not
      "CEO" in one place and "Founder" in another).
- [ ] Ownership % identical in support letter, board consent, I-129 controlling-interest answer, cap table.
- [ ] Employment history on the I-129 lists only employers the user allows; the same employers and
      dates appear in the support letter's qualification section.
- [ ] Company legal name exact (comma, "Inc.") in every document and on the USCIS company account.

## Money
- [ ] Founder contribution amount and month: support letter = board consent = business plan.
- [ ] Bank balance and "as of" date in the support letter match the uploaded statement page.
- [ ] Revenue start month and fundraising month/amount: support letter = business plan.
- [ ] Cost of the position over the period (wage + employer taxes + workers' comp) computed once and
      reused; business plan "uses" table includes USCIS fees paid by the company.
- [ ] No personal bank statements, no funding-commitment letter, unless the user chose to include them.
- [ ] Business plan contingency paragraph exists ("if revenue or financing arrives later than planned")
      and its numbers match the model.

## References and exhibits
- [ ] Every exhibit cited in the support letter is actually uploaded; none is cited that the user
      decided not to submit (expert letter, reference letter).
- [ ] Support letter does not call the offer letter an "employment agreement" if only an offer letter exists.
- [ ] Regulatory citations current: 8 CFR 214.2(h)(4)(iii)(A) (criteria as amended Jan 17 2025),
      8 CFR 214.2(h)(9)(iii)(E) (beneficiary-owner), 20 CFR 655.731 (wage), INA 214(g)(7) (cap exemption).
      Do not cite a court case for a proposition it does not stand for; the OOH "typically need a
      bachelor's" language carries the "normally" criterion on its own.

## Form hygiene
- [ ] All letters signed by hand or e-signature and dated; dates on or before the filing date.
- [ ] Every document is a PDF; the business plan PDF's table of contents and page numbers are correct
      (regenerate from source rather than exporting from Word if the TOC drifted).
- [ ] Editing notes / yellow boxes removed from every document.
- [ ] Filenames: no commas or spaces.
- [ ] Business plan uses future tense for anything not yet shipped; no "prototype is built" unless true.
- [ ] No third-party confidential information anywhere.

Case-law note: do not cite *Innova Solutions v. Baran* (9th Cir. 2020) for the proposition that
Level I wages are compatible with a specialty occupation — it concerned the "normally requires a
degree" criterion for programmers. The OOH's own "typically need a bachelor's degree" language, plus
the 2025 rule text, carries the degree point without case law. If the user wants case citations,
look each one up before using it.

# After the petition is submitted

## Day 0 — receipt

- Case status "Case Was Received and A Receipt Notice Was Sent", receipt `IOE…`. Save the as-filed
  I-129 PDF and the I-797C (Notices tab).
- **Portability** (8 CFR 214.2(h)(2)(i)(H)): a beneficiary in valid H-1B status (or within the
  60-day grace period after leaving the prior employer, 8 CFR 214.1(l)(2)) may begin working for
  the new petitioner once USCIS receives a non-frivolous petition. Work before the petition's
  requested start date is allowed by regulation but the offer letter sets the paid start date; keep
  the two consistent (start payroll on the offer-letter start date).
- Premium processing clock: 15 business days from receipt.

## Before the start date

- Payroll provider (Gusto, Rippling, etc.): the founder as a W-2 employee, hourly rate = LCA wage,
  scheduled hours = LCA hours, first pay period starting on the start date, semi-monthly or
  bi-weekly. Enable state registrations (e.g. California EDD) and workers' compensation.
- Public access file complete (see `lca-prep.md`); add the I-797C.
- Company bank account funded to cover at least the first quarter of payroll + employer taxes.

## The decision

Premium-processing results arrive by email from `ELISdonotreply@uscis.dhs.gov`, often before the
online account updates (account status lags hours to two days; the I-797 PDF appears under Notices
in 1–3 business days; paper notice by mail in 1–2 weeks).

The approval email lists "Consulate/Port of Entry Notified: <consulate>". That line is populated
from the consulate entered on the I-129 and appears on every premium approval; it does not mean
consular processing was substituted for the extension. Confirm with the notice itself:

- **I-797A** with an I-94 tear-off at the bottom (new class H1B, valid to the petition end date)
  → extension of stay approved; the beneficiary is in status under the new employer. This I-94
  replaces the previous one as proof of status.
- **I-797B** (no I-94) → approved for consular notification only; the beneficiary would need to
  obtain a visa / be readmitted to activate it. If this happens when an extension was requested,
  the form answers should be checked and a reopen/correction requested via the premium unit email
  on the approval notice.

An **RFE** arrives the same way; the 15-day clock restarts on the response. Respond through the
online account (Evidence tab → respond to RFE) with the exact documents requested plus a short
cover letter mapping each item.

## After approval

- Save the I-797A; copy into the public access file.
- Payroll already running from the start date; keep pay stubs — they are the maintenance-of-status
  evidence for the next petition.
- Travel: carry the I-797A original and a valid H-1B visa stamp. A visa stamped for the previous
  employer remains valid for entry with the new I-797A until the stamp expires; a new stamp is only
  needed when the old one lapses.
- Calendar: the next petition (extension beyond 18 months) must be filed before the I-94 expiry;
  the beneficiary-owner rule allows up to 3 years for the subsequent petition. File the next LCA
  about 2 months before.
- Driver's license / SSA: no action unless the state license is tied to the I-94 date.

## Changes that need an amended petition

Change of worksite outside the LCA's MSA, material change in duties, hours moving between part-time
and full-time, wage below the LCA wage, or loss of the controlling interest below 50%. A new LCA +
amended I-129 before the change takes effect.

# Before the LCA is certified

The LCA (ETA-9035/9035E) is filed by the employer on DOL's FLAG system (https://flag.dol.gov).
Certification takes 7 business days; the FEIN must first be verified by DOL (upload the IRS EIN
letter under "FEIN verification" — allow a few days the first time). The I-129 cannot be filed
until the LCA is certified and signed.

## Decisions to make before filing the LCA

| Decision | Guidance |
|---|---|
| SOC code | Pick the code whose O*NET tasks match the duties, not the title. Information Security Analysts (15-1212), Software Developers (15-1252), Computer and Information Research Scientists (15-1221) are the usual founder codes. |
| Wage level | Set by the job's **minimum** requirements, not the founder's résumé: bachelor's, no experience, no supervision → Level I. Level I is not a weakness if the JD is written at entry level. |
| Prevailing wage source | OFLC Online Wage Library (https://flag.dol.gov/wage-data/wage-search) for the worksite MSA; print the result page for the public access file. |
| Full-time vs part-time | Part-time (under 35 h/wk) is fine for a founder petition and reduces the wage the company must fund. State hours/week on the LCA; the I-129 must match. |
| Period | Up to 3 years. The I-129 period for a beneficiary-owner is ≤18 months, so the LCA may be longer than the petition. |
| Worksite | Where the founder actually works, home address included (check the lease allows business use without client visits). |
| Employer point of contact | The founder, with the officer title. |

## Posting notice

Before filing the LCA, post a notice of filing at the worksite for 10 consecutive business days (two
conspicuous places, or electronically). Keep a dated copy and a note of where it was posted. For a
home office the notice is posted at the home; keep a photo.

## Public access file (ready within one working day of filing the LCA)

- Certified LCA copy
- Prevailing wage documentation (OWL printout)
- Actual wage memorandum (how the wage was set; for a single employee, the LCA wage)
- Posting notice copies with dates and locations
- Summary of benefits offered to U.S. workers (one page; "same benefits as any employee")
- Later: I-797 approval notice, pay records

Template language for these lives in the business plan outline's appendix; keep the file in the
company's records folder, physical or electronic.

## Wage obligation starts

The required wage must be paid from the earlier of 30 days after the beneficiary becomes available to
work under the petition, or 60 days after admission (20 CFR 655.731(c)(6)). For a founder already in
the U.S. who starts on the petition start date, set payroll so the first pay period begins on that
date.
